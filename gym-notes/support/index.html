Gym Notes

Fastest fix for the usual three

  • Weigh-in not showing? Open the app after weighing — it reads Apple Health when it comes to the front.
  • Report says the photograph could not be read? Stand square, head to knees in frame, in a vest or bare torso, facing the light.
  • Bought Plus but nothing unlocked? Tap Restore on the Plus screen with the same Apple Account.

Where is my notebook?

On your phone, in the app, and nowhere else. There is no account and no server copy, so there is nothing to sign in to and nothing to sync. It is included in your iPhone backup like any other app's data. A lost phone is a lost notebook unless you had a backup; an export is planned and not here yet.

Apple Health isn't filling anything in

  • Check the permission. Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Gym Notes lists what the app may read: weight, body fat, height, date of birth and sex. Turn on the ones you want filled in.
  • Bring the app to the front. It reads Health when it opens or comes back to the screen, not in the background. Weigh in, then open the app.
  • Sessions in Health. A session is written to Health only when you stamp it Completed, and only if you allowed writing. Nothing else is written.

The photograph could not be read

The report says which of four things went wrong, and each has a fix: no one found in the frame, too dark, too far, or too much covered. Stand square to the camera with head to knees in the frame, face a window or a lamp, and wear a vest or nothing on top — loose clothing hides the outline the reading needs. Then Try again on the Profile tab.

It asked before sending my photograph

Once, the first time, because that is the one moment anything leaves your phone: one photograph and four figures go through a relay we run to a model at OpenAI, and the scores come back. Nothing is kept on our side. You can turn this off at any time with Read photographs off this phone in Settings, and the privacy policy says exactly what is sent and who sees it.

The six-month picture

Draw me six months on in the progress report sends the same photograph with your six-month figures and comes back with a picture of you as you may look then. It usually takes under a minute; the page shows the print being traced while it works, and a Stop appears if it runs long. It is a drawing from your photograph and your numbers — not a promise, not a prediction, and never medical. If the model declines to draw from a photograph, the page says so: training clothes, the whole body in frame and plain light give it the best chance. The drawn silhouette stays either way.

A feature is marked Plus

Gym Notes Plus unlocks the features the app marks as such. The plans and prices are shown on the Plus screen before anything is charged — a yearly subscription or a one-time Lifetime purchase.

I bought Plus but it's still locked

Tap Restore on the Plus screen. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with — a purchase belongs to an Apple Account, not to a device. If it still doesn't unlock, email us and say which plan you bought and roughly when.

How do I cancel Plus?

Through Apple, not through us: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and turn off auto-renewal for Gym Notes. You keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel anything. A Lifetime purchase has nothing to cancel.

How do I delete everything?

Erase at the foot of Settings removes the whole notebook, photographs included, permanently. Deleting the app does the same. There is no copy anywhere to ask us to delete.

Still stuck

Email support@bastonapps.com. Please include:

  • The app version (Settings, at the bottom)
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead

One person answers this address, so replies take a day or two, but they are replies from someone who can actually change the app.
