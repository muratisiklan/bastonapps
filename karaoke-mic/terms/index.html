Hearing and volume

Read this one. The app sends your microphone straight to a loudspeaker, which means two things can get loud very fast: the level fader, and acoustic feedback when the microphone starts to hear the speaker it is feeding. The app includes a noise gate and warns you when it detects feedback beginning, but these are aids, not guarantees.

Start with the volume low and raise it. Keep the phone away from the speaker. Don't hold a speaker or phone against anyone's ear while the app is live. Loud sound can damage hearing permanently, and you are responsible for the volume you choose and for the equipment you connect. If you hear ringing or discomfort, stop.
