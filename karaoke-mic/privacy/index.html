2. Audio and microphone data

Audio captured by the microphone is not recorded, stored, or transmitted off your device. It is processed in memory in real time and played back to the audio output you have selected, and is then discarded. The Application contains no recording feature, no upload mechanism, and no server to which audio could be sent.

The Application derives a numerical measure of signal level from the incoming audio many times per second. This measure drives the on-screen level indicator, the noise gate, and the detection of acoustic feedback. It exists only in volatile memory for the duration of the audio session and is neither stored nor transmitted.

iOS requires your permission before the Application may access the microphone. Permission may be withdrawn at any time in Settings → Karaoke Mic → Microphone.

The microphone is active only while the Application is in the foreground. The Application does not declare the background audio capability, so the audio session ends when the Application is backgrounded or the device is locked.

Where audio from another application is playing, iOS combines it with the microphone signal on the output path. The Application has no access to that audio, cannot identify its content, and is not informed of its source.

Purpose: selection, delivery, measurement, and frequency capping of advertising, and prevention of fraudulent activity.

Legal basis: your consent, under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and the corresponding provisions of KVKK. Consent is obtained through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt and, where the framework applies, through a consent form presented by Google's certified consent management platform. Where consent for personalised advertising is not given, advertising is served on a non-personalised basis and your advertising identifier is not used for targeting.

Under Apple's App Store rules this processing constitutes tracking, and it is declared as such in the Application's App Store privacy disclosures as Device ID and Advertising Data, collected for third-party advertising and not linked to your identity.

3.2 Subscription and purchase data

Where you purchase a Premium subscription, payment is processed by Apple. We do not receive or have access to your payment card details, billing address, or Apple Account credentials.

We use RevenueCat, Inc. to determine whether a subscription is active. RevenueCat assigns your installation a randomly generated anonymous identifier and processes the associated transaction and entitlement status received from Apple.

Purpose: verifying entitlement, making paid features available, and enabling restoration of a purchase on another device.

Legal basis: performance of a contract to which you are party, under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3.3 Consent records

Where a consent form is presented under Google's consent management platform, your response is recorded so that it can be applied on subsequent launches and so that you may reopen and change it.

Legal basis: compliance with a legal obligation, under Article 6(1)(c) GDPR, namely the obligation to demonstrate that valid consent was obtained.

This information remains on your device, is not transmitted to us or to any third party, is not accessible to us, and is removed when the Application is deleted.
