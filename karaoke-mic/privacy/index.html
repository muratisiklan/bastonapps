Privacy Policy

Karaoke Mic · Effective 31 July 2026

The short version

  • Your audio is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.
  • The microphone works only while the app is open on screen.
  • There is no account. We never learn your name or email.
  • Ads use your device's advertising identifier. You can refuse this.
  • Subscribing to Premium removes the ads and the ad software entirely.

Karaoke Mic is made by Baston Apps. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with information about you. It covers the iOS app only.

Your microphone and your voice

This is the part most people want to know, so it comes first. Audio from your microphone is never recorded to a file, never stored, and never transmitted off your device. It is captured, processed, and played back out to your chosen speaker in real time, then discarded. There is no recording feature in the app, no upload, and no server for audio to be sent to.

The app does measure how loud the incoming audio is, many times a second, as a single number. That number moves the level meter on screen, opens and closes the noise gate, and detects when the microphone has started to hear the speaker and is about to squeal. Those numbers exist only in memory while the app is running and are never saved or sent anywhere.

iOS asks your permission before the app can use the microphone. If you refuse, the app cannot do the one thing it exists to do, but it will not stop working in any other way.

The microphone is live only while the app is open in front of you. Karaoke Mic does not request the background audio capability, so switching to another app or locking your phone ends the session — iOS suspends the app and the microphone closes with it. There is no arrangement under which this app is listening while you are doing something else.

If you are playing music from another app, that keeps going while Karaoke Mic is open, and the two are mixed together by iOS on their way to your speaker. Karaoke Mic has no access to that audio, cannot read what you are playing, and is not told which app it came from.

What the app collects

Advertising

The free version of the app shows a banner advertisement supplied by Google AdMob. To do that, Google receives your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) along with technical information such as your device model, iOS version, coarse location inferred from your IP address, and how you interacted with the ad. Google uses this to select ads and to measure them.

Under Apple's rules this counts as tracking, which is why the app asks for your permission before it happens. It is also why the App Store listing declares that the app collects a Device ID and Advertising Data, used for third-party advertising, and not linked to your identity.

If you decline, ads still appear but are non-personalised: Google is not given your advertising identifier for targeting purposes.

Purchases

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple — we never see your card details, billing address, or Apple Account. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is currently active. RevenueCat assigns your installation a random, anonymous identifier for this purpose. It is not linked to your name, email, or any other identifier, and we cannot use it to work out who you are.

Settings stored on your device

Your chosen voice and its settings, microphone sensitivity, output volume, echo reduction, whether the screen stays awake, haptics preference, app icon, whether you have seen the introduction, and whether you have been asked to rate the app are saved on your device so the app remembers them. This information stays on your phone, is not readable by us, and is deleted when you delete the app.

What the app never collects

  • Recordings of your voice, or any audio at all
  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Contacts, photos, calendar, or health data
  • Precise location
  • Anything at all if you have subscribed to Premium and never see an ad

Your choices

  • Tracking. iOS asks once whether the app may track you. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or turn it off for every app at once.
  • Consent, in Europe. If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form from Google's consent platform before any ad software starts. You can reopen it later from Settings → Privacy choices inside the app and change your mind. That option only appears where this consent framework applies.
  • Removing ads entirely. Subscribing to Premium is not just an option to hide the banner. When you are a Premium subscriber the advertising software is never started at all, so no ad request is ever made and no identifier is ever shared.
  • Resetting your advertising identifier. iOS lets you reset it, which severs any history associated with the old one.

Who else is involved

The app relies on three third parties, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — advertising. Privacy policy · How Google uses data from partner apps
  • RevenueCat — subscription status. Privacy policy
  • Apple — app distribution and payment. Privacy policy

We do not use any analytics service, crash reporter, or social network software development kit.

Children

Karaoke Mic is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area), and we do not knowingly collect information from them. The app is not submitted to the App Store's Kids Category. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us and we will help.

Where information goes

Because we hold no personal data ourselves, there is nothing for us to transfer between countries. Google, RevenueCat, and Apple operate internationally and handle any transfers of the limited data described above under their own policies and safeguards.

How long it is kept

We keep nothing, so there is nothing for us to delete. Settings stored on your device disappear when you delete the app. Data held by Google, RevenueCat, and Apple is retained according to their own published policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export personal data held about you, to object to its processing, or to withdraw consent. Since Karaoke Mic has no account system and we hold no identifying information, in practice the effective controls are the ones listed under Your choices above, plus deleting the app. If you want to exercise a right and are not sure how, write to us and we will do what we can — including telling you honestly if the data you are asking about is not something we ever had.

To exercise rights against Google, RevenueCat, or Apple directly, use the contacts in their policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, this page is updated and the effective date at the top changes with it. Material changes will also be noted in the app's App Store release notes.
