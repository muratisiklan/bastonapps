Three things, in order

  1. 01

    Pair your speaker

    Any Bluetooth speaker, soundbar, or AirPlay device. Pair it in iOS Settings first — the app shows you which output it's using and lets you switch.

  2. 02

    Allow the microphone

    Asked once, on first launch. Without it there's nothing to send to the speaker.

  3. 03

    Tap and talk

    One tap goes live, another mutes you — muted is the resting state, so you're never live by accident. Set the level with the fader, pick a voice, and the whole screen takes on its colour.

What it is, and what it isn't

It is

  • A live microphone that plays through a speaker you already own
  • Eleven character voices, applied to your voice as you speak
  • Happy to share the speaker — keep playing music from any other app and sing over the top of it
  • A level fader, a noise gate, and feedback warnings when the mic starts to hear itself
  • Live only while it's open, so nothing listens in the background
  • Four app icons you can switch between
  • Free, with an optional Premium subscription

It isn't

  • A karaoke library — it supplies no songs, lyrics, or backing tracks; bring your own from the music app you already pay for
  • A recorder. Nothing is captured or saved, by design
  • A scoring game
  • Latency-free — Bluetooth adds a short delay no app can remove

Your voice doesn't go anywhere

Audio is processed on the device and played straight out again. It is never recorded to a file, never stored, and never transmitted — not to us, not to anyone. The only thing the app measures is loudness, as a number, to move the meter and open the noise gate.
